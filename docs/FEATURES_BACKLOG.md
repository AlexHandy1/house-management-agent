## Search/RAG over news feed articles

Build search/RAG tool functionality over the articles pulled in from the news feed, so the agent can reason over and use all that info directly rather than being limited to whatever is surfaced inline. Would let the agent retrieve relevant past articles on demand and ground its responses in the full archive, not just recent/explicitly-passed items.
